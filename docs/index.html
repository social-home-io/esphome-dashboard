Social Home dashboard

Turn a touch panel into a Social Home wall dashboard. Plug the panel into this computer with USB-C, pick it below and press Install. After flashing, the installer asks for your Wi-Fi.

Supported panels

  • Loading the device list…

Your browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.

After installing

  • No USB cable handy? Set Wi-Fi from a phone with Improv over Bluetooth (Home Assistant finds the panel, or use improv-wifi.com) and tap Allow on the panel.
  • Home Assistant discovers the panel through ESPHome. Adopting it is optional: the dashboard works without Home Assistant.
  • Updates arrive over the air from this site.
